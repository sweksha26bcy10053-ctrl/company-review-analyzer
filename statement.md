-Problem Statement
Companies receive many customer reviews and complaints about their products and services. Reading every review manually can take time and make it difficult to identify common problems.
The Company Review and Complaint Analysis System is a Python-based application that analyzes customer reviews using simple keyword matching. It identifies positive points, negative points, affected areas, and provides suggestions for improvement.
The system also allows analyzed reviews to be saved and viewed later.
+- Objective
The main objectives of the project are:
- Analyze customer reviews and complaints.
- Identify positive and negative words.
- Identify areas such as service, product, delivery, price, app, and staff.
- Provide simple suggestions based on identified problems.
- Save reviews for future viewing.
- Demonstrate the use of basic Python programming concepts.
+ Proposed Solution
The system accepts a company name and a customer review as input. It analyzes the review using predefined positive and negative keywords. Based on the analysis, it displays the overall result, good points, problems, affected areas, and suggestions.
The analyzed review is then stored in a text file so that previously saved reviews can be viewed later.
